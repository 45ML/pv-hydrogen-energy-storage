# SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy

This project aligns with United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 7, particularly Target 7.2, which calls for increasing the share of renewable energy in the global energy mix by 2030. The PV–hydrogen microgrid explores solar generation, conversion of electrical energy into stored hydrogen, and subsequent electricity generation through fuel-cell operation.

For the electric-drives lab, the connection is the study of renewable sources and energy storage for a future converter-fed DC supply. The completed simulations demonstrate changes in hydrogen inventory and irradiance-dependent calculated PV power. With suitable supervisory control, this arrangement could support electrical loads when solar availability changes. Affordability, reduced emissions and reliable autonomous supply remain topics for future assessment.
